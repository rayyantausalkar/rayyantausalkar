## Featured Projects

### Graphite: AI Assistant for Engineering Blueprints

Engineers spend hundreds of hours reading complex plant drawings by hand to check equipment and how everything connects. One missed connection can lead to costly mistakes on site. Graphite is an AI system that helps engineers understand these drawings quickly.

### RHIPay: Cross-Border Payments Platform

Sending money abroad is often slow, costly, and risky for personal data. RHIPay shows how international payments can be fast, low-cost, and private, while still following banking rules.
